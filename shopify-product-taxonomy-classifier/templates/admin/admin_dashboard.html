Intelligent Product Classification
AI-Based Shopify Product Taxonomy Classification

The Product Taxonomy Intelligence System is a Python and Django-based platform designed to automatically classify large product catalogues according to the Shopify Product Taxonomy. The system analyzes available product information including product title, description, product type, brand, attributes, and product images to determine the most appropriate taxonomy category.

The platform is designed to process thousands of products efficiently while handling incomplete product information, missing descriptions, unavailable images, and classification failures. Each classification receives a confidence score, and products with uncertain results are automatically identified for manual review.

  • AI-Powered Shopify Product Category Classification

  • Automatic Category Attribute and Value Detection

  • Confidence Score and Alternative Category Suggestions

  • Batch Processing with Manual Review and Approval
